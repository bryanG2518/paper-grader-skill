# Citation and formatting pass (APA 7th)

Layer this on top of a content rubric. It is worth roughly 10% of most grades, so
never lead with it. Citation errors are the cheapest points a student ever
loses, and a mismatched reference list is a flag graders look for specifically.

Adapt the mechanics if the course uses MLA, Chicago, or Harvard; the cross-checks
in the last section apply regardless of style.

## Cross-checks that catch the expensive errors

Do these before checking any punctuation. They find missing-source problems, which
cost far more than format errors.

1. **Every in-text citation appears in the reference list.** List any that don't.
2. **Every reference is cited at least once in the body.** Uncited references look padded.
3. **Years match** between the in-text citation and the reference entry.
4. **Author spelling matches** across both.
5. **Direct quotes carry page or paragraph numbers.** Missing locators on quotes are marked consistently.
6. **Source recency:** for business, technology, or policy topics, sources older than about five years need a reason to be there.
7. **Source quality:** count how many references are peer-reviewed, primary, or industry-standard versus web pages and blogs. Report the ratio.

## APA 7 mechanics

**In-text**
- Parenthetical: `(Kotler & Keller, 2022)`, ampersand inside parentheses
- Narrative: `Kotler and Keller (2022) argue`, spelled-out "and" in running text
- Three or more authors: `(Chen et al., 2021)` from the first citation onward
- Direct quote: `(Chen et al., 2021, p. 47)`
- Quotes over 40 words: block quote, indented half an inch, no quotation marks, citation after the final period

**Reference list**
- New page, heading "References", centered and bold
- Alphabetical by first author's surname
- Hanging indent of half an inch
- Journal article: Author, A. A. (Year). Title of article in sentence case. *Journal Name in Title Case*, *volume*(issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxx
- Book: Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work in sentence case*. Publisher.
- Web page: Author or organization. (Year, Month Day). *Title*. Site Name. URL
- Include a DOI whenever one exists

**Document formatting**
- Double-spaced throughout, including the reference list
- One-inch margins
- 12pt Times New Roman, 11pt Calibri, or 11pt Arial
- Page number in the top right of every page
- Title page with title, author, institution, course, instructor, and due date
- Level 1 headings centered and bold; level 2 flush left and bold

## Reporting format

Don't itemize thirty comma errors. Group them:

> Citation style: 4 issues — three in-text citations use "and" inside parentheses where APA requires "&" (pp. 3, 5, 8), and one direct quote is missing a page number (p. 6).

Then flag anything from the cross-check section separately, because a citation with
no matching reference is a content problem, not a formatting one.
