# Grade bands and level expectations

## Score to letter

| Score | Letter |
|---|---|
| 97–100 | A+ |
| 93–96 | A |
| 90–92 | A− |
| 87–89 | B+ |
| 83–86 | B |
| 80–82 | B− |
| 77–79 | C+ |
| 73–76 | C |
| 70–72 | C− |
| 60–69 | D |
| below 60 | F |

Most graduate programs treat a C as a de facto failing grade for degree progress. If
a graduate paper scores below 80, say plainly that it's a problem, not just a number.

## What each band means in practice

**A range:** The paper takes a position, defends it against the strongest objection,
and the evidence does the work. A grader reading it has nothing to write in the
margin except agreement. An A is not a clean B. Polish never converts a B paper.

**B range:** Competent and complete. Everything asked for is present. What's missing
is a reason for the reader to care: it describes rather than argues, or argues
without engaging anything that might contradict it. This is where most submitted
drafts land, and moving to an A almost always requires adding analysis, not editing
sentences.

**C range:** A required component is missing, underdeveloped, or wrong. The paper
covers the topic without doing the task.

**D and below:** Doesn't do what the assignment asked.

## Level expectations

**Undergraduate:** Correct comprehension, a defensible claim, adequate sourcing.
Original insight earns the A; competent execution earns the B.

**Graduate / MBA:** Comprehension is assumed and earns nothing. The A requires
synthesis across sources, a defensible position under pressure, engagement with
counterarguments, and, in business courses, quantification. A well-written summary
of the literature is a B. Say this explicitly when it applies; students carrying
undergrad habits into graduate work are the most common case this skill sees.

**Doctoral:** Add a contribution to the conversation: a gap identified, a method
justified against alternatives, and awareness of the work's own limitations.

## Projected grades

The projected grade is what the paper earns after the fixes listed. Two rules:

1. **Don't promise an A the paper can't reach.** If the core argument is weak and the
   deadline is tomorrow, the honest projection is a B+ and the honest advice is which
   two fixes buy the most points per hour.
2. **Show the arithmetic.** "Fixing the evidence gaps in sections 3 and 4 moves that
   category from 72 to 88, which is +4 overall, a B to a B+. Adding the
   counterargument section is what gets you into the A range."
